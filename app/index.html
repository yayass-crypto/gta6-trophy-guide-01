    The road to the platinum — about 200 hours

    Difficulty 4/10. One playthrough, 55 offline trophies, 0 online. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so play clean. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, which is why the save advice below matters.

    ⚠ The one missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots, 10 locations). Meditating with both characters at a spot counts double, and the final meditation needs both characters together (possible after Chapter 2, “Coffee Break”).

    Chapter 1, do not skip: after the mission “Down to Earth”, Lucia stands in the kitchen in the morning at Jason’s house — follow her to the balcony and accept the meditation when prompted. It is unclear whether that spot can be revisited later. Track it under Progress → Collections → Exploration → Meditations. For 100% completion only 15 of 18 spots are needed; this trophy wants all 18.

    Save advice — 15 manual slots + 1 autosave

    Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter, and one more right before the final mission, “Starting From Zero”. With no mission replay and no New Game+, a pre-finale save is your only way back into the late story. Rotate the slots so an old chapter save is never your only copy.

    After the story, open Progress → Stats → 100% Completion and work the Stage 2 checklist from there.
