# Leonida Legends — GTA VI Trophy Guide & Tracker

An offline guide and tracker for all **55 official Grand Theft Auto VI trophies** on PlayStation 5: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze — and **0 online trophies**, so everything here is single-player.

## What it is

- **A checklist that works.** Tick each trophy as you earn it; your progress saves on your device (localStorage), with a progress bar and per-grade counts.
- **A guide for every trophy, in its own words.** Each guide says what to do, where and how, and when it can be missed — and cites its source beside it (PowerPyx, with KeenGamer and GTA Zone where they add to the list).
- **A platinum roadmap.** Three stages — Story (60–80 h), 100% checklist (~100 h), Strangers & clean-up (~10 h) — totalling about 200 hours at difficulty 4/10, with save advice (15 manual slots + 1 autosave; keep a save at the start and end of each chapter).
- **One honest missable warning.** Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy that can be missed: meditate at all 18 spots, and do not skip the Chapter 1 balcony meditation after “Down to Earth”.
- **Spoiler protection.** Story trophies stay hidden (“Hidden story trophy — earn it to reveal”) until earned, unless you switch spoilers off.
- **Filters, search, export/import.** Filter by kind (Story / Collectibles / Activities / Missable / Online), grade, or earned state; search by name, mission or place; export your progress as JSON and import it back.

Five trophies are still unconfirmed by the published guides (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin). This guide says so plainly for each of them instead of inventing a method.

## How to open it

No build step, no network, no account. Serve the `app/` folder with any static server and open `index.html`, or open the file directly in a browser:

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```
